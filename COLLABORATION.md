# Research and Collaboration / 研究与合作

UOHF is being developed as a governed semantic and rule foundation for representing human function across internal and external demands, whole-person state, evidence, action, feedback, and longitudinal change.

UOHF 正在被建设为一套受治理的语义与规则基础，用于表达内外需求下的人体功能、完整人体状态、证据、行动、反馈和长期变化。

## Current collaboration priorities / 当前重点合作方向

- ontology engineering and knowledge representation;
- whole-person and multiscale physiological modeling;
- semantic interoperability with established biomedical and clinical standards;
- rehabilitation, movement science, exercise, and human performance;
- longitudinal human-function and health-state modeling;
- auditable, evidence-bounded, and safety-constrained health AI;
- task-centered assessment, action, feedback, and reassessment systems;
- formal validation, competency questions, mappings, and public ontology-release methods.

- 本体工程与知识表示；
- 完整人体和多尺度生理建模；
- 与既有生物医学及临床标准的语义互操作；
- 康复、运动科学、训练与人体表现；
- 长期人体功能与健康状态建模；
- 可审计、受证据边界和安全约束的健康 AI；
- 任务中心的评估、行动、反馈与复评系统；
- 形式验证、能力问题、映射及公共本体发布方法。

## Useful forms of contribution / 有价值的参与方式

External researchers and practitioners can contribute through:

1. conceptual critique of definitions and category boundaries;
2. comparison with existing ontologies, classifications, physiological models, and clinical terminologies;
3. proposed competency questions and counterexamples;
4. mapping proposals that preserve the identity and scope of external standards;
5. review of machine-readable public subsets when released;
6. domain-specific case studies with explicit evidence and professional-scope boundaries;
7. reproducibility, validation, and interoperability testing.

外部研究者和专业人员可以通过以下方式参与：

1. 审查定义、类别和边界；
2. 与现有本体、分类、生理模型和临床术语进行比较；
3. 提出能力问题、反例和失败案例；
4. 提出不损害外部标准原有身份与范围的映射方案；
5. 审查后续发布的机器可读公共子集；
6. 提供具有明确证据和专业权限边界的领域案例；
7. 开展可复现性、验证和互操作测试。

## Claim boundaries / 主张边界

Collaboration does not imply that UOHF currently has:

- completed BFO conformance;
- completed OWL axiomatization;
- external expert consensus;
- clinical outcome validation;
- a completed molecular-to-person simulator;
- authority to diagnose or prescribe treatment autonomously.

参与讨论或合作不表示 UOHF 当前已经：

- 完成 BFO 全面符合；
- 完成 OWL 公理化；
- 形成外部专家共识；
- 完成临床结局验证；
- 建成分子到整个人的完整模拟器；
- 获得自动诊断或自动治疗的权限。
