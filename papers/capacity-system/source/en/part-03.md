## 7. Sustained Task Endurance Capacity
Version 1.0 intentionally defines no forced child capacities under this core capacity. Intensity, duration, frequency, pace, repetition, recovery interval, and task cost are treated as demand conditions or evidence dimensions to avoid renaming parameters as parallel capacities. Basis [1,2,3,4,5,6].
